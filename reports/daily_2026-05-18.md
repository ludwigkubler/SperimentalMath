### Statement

For a connected 3-regular graph G=(V,E) with odd charge ω:V→F_2 (Σ_v ω(v)≡1 mod 2), build a Z/2-gauge representative ψ∈F_2^E by BFS from a fixed root v_0, setting each tree edge so that ⊕_{e∋v}ψ(e)=ω(v) at every non-root v (the unsatisfiability deficit lands at v_0); form the signed adjacency A^σ with σ(e)=(−1)^{ψ(e)} and the signed Laplacian L^σ=D−A^σ, and define μ(G,ω):=λ_min(L^σ), which is gauge-invariant under ψ↦ψ⊕δf and equals 0 iff Σω is even. We conjecture an absolute constant c>0 such that for every unsatisfiable T(G,ω) on 3-regular G, log_2 t*(T(G,ω)) ≥ c · μ(G,ω) · |V|, with pre-registered c=0.05. A single 3-regular instance (G,ω) with log_2 t*(T(G,ω)) < 0.05 · μ(G,ω) · |V| falsifies the conjecture.

### Rationale

The Tseitin obstruction is exactly the Z/2 holonomy class [ω]∈H^1(G;F_2), and μ(G,ω) is the spectral norm of this obstruction in the discrete Bochner–Laplacian sense — large (Θ(1)) on expanders where holonomy is globally rigid, and small (Θ(1/|V|^2)) on tree-like graphs where the obstruction concentrates locally. The mechanism funnels the Ben-Sasson–Wigderson expansion-based exp(Ω(boundary expansion)) lower bound through a single gauge-invariant scalar via the signed Cheeger inequality h^σ(G)≥√(2dμ), making the standard expansion bound a corollary of a finer structural invariant. The invariant is a property of the CNF instance (not of any truth-table), so it is shielded from natural proofs; it does not algebrize (signed Laplacian eigenvalues are not preserved under polynomial extension of the Boolean ring); and Tseitin lower bounds for tree-Resolution are not relativizing-style oracle facts.
