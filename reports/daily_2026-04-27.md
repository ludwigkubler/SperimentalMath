### Statement

Let f be a TropicalPolynomial over a finite domain D = {-N, ..., N} ⊂ Z (min-plus semiring). ### Statement

Let M ∈ {-1,+1}^{N×N} with N=2^n have singular values σ_1 ≥ σ_2 ≥ ... ≥ σ_N, and let ρ(M)=σ_2/σ_1 ∈ [0,1]. ### Rationale
