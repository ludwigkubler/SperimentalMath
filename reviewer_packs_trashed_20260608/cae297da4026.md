## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Persistent homology of Vietoris-Rips filtrations on Hamming point clouds
**Field B** (complexity object): Discrepancy-method lower bounds for randomized communication complexity of partial Boolean matrices

**Statement**:

> For an n×n sign matrix M_f obtained from a Boolean function f:{0,1}^k×{0,1}^k→{±1} (n=2^k, k≤4), embed the rows as a point cloud R⊂{0,1}^k under Hamming distance and compute the bottleneck distance d_B(Dgm_0(R),Dgm_0(C)) between the 0-dimensional persistence diagrams of rows and columns. Then disc(M_f) ≤ 2^{-c·k}·exp(α·d_B) for absolute constants c>0, α>0; equivalently, log(1/disc(M_f)) ≥ c·k − α·d_B(Dgm_0(R),Dgm_0(C)). A single (f,k) with log(1/disc) < c·k − α·d_B refutes the bound for any fixed admissible (c,α)=(1/2, ln 2).

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Discrepancy is governed by how 'spread out' the ±1 pattern is across rectangles, and persistent-homology bottleneck distance between row- and column-clouds quantifies the asymmetry of cluster-merging scales — a feature invisible to spectral norms but plausibly tracking the rectangle-balance that controls disc. The bridge is novel because persistent homology has been applied to neural nets and TDA-of-data but essentially never to communication matrices, and the 0-dim Rips diagram is computable from a Hamming distance matrix without algebraic machinery. If the bound holds, it would give a geometric, basis-independent lower-bound certificate complementary to γ_2 and approximate-rank methods.
