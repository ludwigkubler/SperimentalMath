## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Additive combinatorics in F_2^n: the Plünnecke-Ruzsa doubling constant K(A)=|A+A|/|A| and its log version σ(A)=log_2|A+A|-log_2|A| of a finite set A⊆F_2^n (Freiman-Ruzsa / Bogolyubov / Sanders' polynomial Bogolyubov tradition). The mathematical branch is harmonic / arithmetic combinatorics on abelian groups, distinct from polyhedral / Newton-polytope geometry, and rarely deployed in SoS lower bounds for CSPs.
**Field B** (complexity object): Sum-of-Squares refutation degree d_SoS(F) of unsatisfiable 3-XOR instances F over GF(2), accessed via the Grigoriev-Schoenebeck combinatorial proxy g*(F) = min{|T| : ⊕_{i∈T} a_i = 0 ∈ F_2^n and ⊕_{i∈T} b_i = 1 ∈ F_2}, which sandwiches SoS degree to within ±1 by the Schoenebeck (2008) characterization for 3-XOR.

**Statement**:

> For every unsatisfiable 3-XOR instance F = ((a_i,b_i))_{i=1..m} on n ≤ 20 variables with constraint multiset A = {a_1,...,a_m} ⊆ F_2^n, the Schoenebeck combinatorial SoS-degree satisfies g*(F) ≥ σ(A) + 1, where σ(A) = log_2|A+A| - log_2|set(A)|. Equivalently, |A+A| ≤ |set(A)|·2^{g*(F)-1}. A single (F, A) violating this inequality refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Schoenebeck's theorem says small SoS degree forces a short additive dependency among constraint vectors; Plünnecke-Ruzsa says short additive dependencies push A toward an affine-subspace structure with low doubling. The bridge predicts a monotone, never-before-quantified link between structurelessness (large doubling) of the constraint vectors and the unavoidable length of a Schoenebeck certificate, transferring Freiman-Ruzsa intuition into proof complexity.
