## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Combinatorial commutative algebra: projective dimension pd(R_Δ) of the Stanley-Reisner ring R_Δ = GF(2)[x_1,…,x_n]/I_Δ accessed via Hochster's formula β_{i,T}(R_Δ) = dim tilde H_{|T|-i-1}(Δ|_T; GF(2)), in the Stanley–Hochster–Reisner–Terai tradition (Miller–Sturmfels, Combinatorial Commutative Algebra). Rarely deployed against circuit/formula complexity; distinct from polytope/Newton-polytope or matroid-rank approaches.
**Field B** (complexity object): Monotone Karchmer–Wigderson game depth D_m(f) of monotone Boolean functions f:{0,1}^n→{0,1}, equivalently monotone formula depth in the {AND,OR}-basis via the Karchmer–Wigderson equivalence — the central object of Razborov-style monotone NC^1 lower bounds.

**Statement**:

> Define Δ_f := {S ⊆ [n] : f(1_S)=0}, the simplicial complex of non-1-certificates of a monotone f, and let Pd(f) := max{|T|−j−1 : T ⊆ [n], j ≥ −1, tilde H_j(Δ_f|_T; GF(2)) ≠ 0} (with Pd(f)=0 if all reduced homologies vanish). Then for every monotone f, the monotone-KW protocol depth satisfies D_m(f) ≥ Pd(f). One counterexample (a monotone f with D_m(f) < Pd(f)) refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> A monotone-KW protocol decomposes the rectangle MIN(f)×MAX(¬f) into coordinate-labeled monochromatic rectangles; this rectangular decomposition is the combinatorial shadow of a free resolution of I_{Δ_f}, whose length is exactly pd(R_{Δ_f}) by Auslander–Buchsbaum. Hochster's formula reduces pd to vanishing of reduced GF(2)-homology on induced subcomplexes, an invariant that is rarely linked to KW games (prior monotone bounds use fooling sets, lattice rank, or Razborov approximators). Because the conjecture targets monotone classes only, it sidesteps the natural-proofs barrier (which constrains P/poly with arbitrary basis under crypto assumptions, not monotone NC^1).
