### Statement

For a monotone Boolean function f on N variables, let M(f) ⊂ {0,1}^N be its set of minterms (minimal satisfying assignments viewed as 0/1 vectors) and let K(f) := conv(M(f) ∪ {0}) ⊆ [0,1]^N.
