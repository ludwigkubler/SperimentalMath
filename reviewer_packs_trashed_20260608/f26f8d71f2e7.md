**Statement**:

> Let f: Z_8 → R be a tropical polynomial and g = f ⊗_min f its min-plus self-convolution on Z_8. Let TFT_β denote the Maslov-dequantized Fourier transform at inverse temperature β, defined by TFT_β(h)[k] = -(1/β)·log| Σ_{j=0}^{7} exp(-β·h[j])·ω^{jk} | with ω = exp(2πi/8), and MFC_β(h) = min_{k≠0} |TFT_β(h)[k]|. Define Δ(f,β) := |MFC_β(g) − 2·MFC_β(f)|. We conjecture that for every f with entries in [0,1]^8 and every β ∈ {5,10,20,50,100,200}, Δ(f,β) ≤ 10·log(8)/β; a single (f,β) pair with Δ(f,β) > 10·log(8)/β falsifies the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Maslov dequantization gives max_i a_i = (1/β)·log Σ exp(β a_i) + O(log N / β), so any tropical identity that becomes exact at β=∞ should pick up an O(log N / β) defect at finite β.
