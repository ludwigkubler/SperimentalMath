**Statement**:

> Let F be a monotone DNF formula representing the k-CLIQUE function on n-vertex graphs. Let M(F) be the simple binary matroid whose ground set is the set of terms of F, and whose independent sets are subsets of pairwise edge-disjoint terms. Then the girth of M(F) — the size of the smallest circuit (minimal dependent set) in M(F) — is at least k. Furthermore, if a monotone DNF has matroid girth < k, it cannot compute k-CLIQUE on all n-vertex graphs for n ≥ 3k.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

High girth forces structural complexity in the DNF that reflects the necessity of large support for covering all k-cliques without false positives. This connects extremal combinatorics in matroids to proof complexity in monotone circuits.
