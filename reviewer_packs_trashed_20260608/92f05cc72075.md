## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Combinatorics of 0/1 matrix permanents (Ryser-formula variance over random row-sign perturbations)
**Field B** (complexity object): Nisan-Wigderson pseudorandom generator output bias against small DNF distinguishers

**Statement**:

> Let D be an (ell,a)-NW design on n variables encoded as an m x n 0/1 incidence matrix M (rows = subsets S_i, columns = universe). Define V(M) := log2(1 + Var_{eps in {+/-1}^n}[ perm(M_eps) ]) / m, where M_eps is M with column j scaled by eps_j and perm is the 0/1 permanent on the (m x m) submatrix induced by the m heaviest columns. We conjecture that for every hard truth table f : {0,1}^ell -> {0,1} of formula-leaf-count L(f) >= 2^{ell/3}, the maximum bias of NW_{D,f} against width-w DNF distinguishers satisfies bias(NW_{D,f}, w) <= 2^{-c * V(M) * m / w} for an absolute constant c > 0, so V(M) is a constructive lower bound certificate for PRG quality.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Permanent variance over sign perturbations measures how 'spread' the design's row-overlap pattern is, which is exactly the combinatorial quantity controlling pairwise-intersection cancellation in the NW hybrid argument; existing analyses use only |S_i cap S_j| <= a, but the variance aggregates higher-order interference. Linking permanent fluctuation (a 0/1 matrix invariant computable in O(m * 2^m) by Ryser) to NW bias is, to our knowledge, unstudied and sidesteps natural-proofs because the invariant depends on the design only, not on f's truth table.
