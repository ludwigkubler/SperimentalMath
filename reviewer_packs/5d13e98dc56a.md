## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Matroid Theory
**Field B** (complexity object): Monotone DNF Formulas

**Statement**:

> For any monotone DNF formula Φ with m clauses over n variables, define μ(Φ) = max_{A ⊆ [n], |A| ≤ k} (rank(Φ_A) - |A|), where Φ_A is the restriction of Φ to A and rank(Φ_A) is the maximum number of disjoint clauses in Φ_A. Then μ(Φ) ≤ log n for all Φ with m = poly(n), and μ(k-CLIQUE) ≥ n^{1/2} for any k ≥ 2.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Matroid rank functions are submodular, and their growth rates could expose structural limitations in DNF representations. The k-CLIQUE problem's clause structure inherently requires high rank values due to combinatorial explosion in subset dependencies, creating a natural separation between general DNF and clique-specific formulas.
