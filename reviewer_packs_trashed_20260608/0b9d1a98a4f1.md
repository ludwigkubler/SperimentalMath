**Field B** (complexity object): Read-Twice Branching Programs

**Statement**:

> For a read-twice branching program P computing IP_2 (inner product mod 2), let μ_P be the empirical spectral distribution of its transition matrix. The free entropy ϕ(μ_P) = -∫ log|z| dμ_P(z) satisfies ϕ(μ_P) = Θ(log n) for trivial P (e.g., constant functions), but ϕ(μ_P) ≥ Ω(n) for nontrivial P. The gap is witnessed by the spectral radius λ_P = max{|z| : z ∈ spec(μ_P)} with λ_P ≥ 1 + Ω(1/n) for nontrivial P.

Read-twice BPs for IP_2 exhibit distinct spectral signatures: trivial programs concentrate measure near 1, while nontrivial ones spread it across eigenvalues, creating a detectable free entropy gap.
