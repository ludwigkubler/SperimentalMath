**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> The Hermite-Sturm count of moment-matrix eigenvalues in (−1,1) is a real-semialgebraic invariant of the SOS pseudo-distribution that is preserved under monomial relabelings and odd-degree real-affine reparametrizations, hence under simple polynomial reductions of CSPs.
