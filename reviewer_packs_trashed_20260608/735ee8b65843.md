**Statement**:

> For any explicit Boolean function f ∈ P with n variables, its additive energy E(f) = |{(x,y,z,w) ∈ {0,1}^n⁴ : f(x) + f(y) = f(z) + f(w)}| satisfies E(f) ≤ O(n²). Conversely, for any AC⁰ circuit computing f, E(f) ≥ Ω(2^{2n}/n²).

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Additive energy captures the structured regularity of a function's truth table.
