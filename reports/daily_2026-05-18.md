### Statement

For an unsatisfiable 3-CNF F with m clauses on n variables, let s_{i,v} ∈ {±1} be the polarity sign of variable v in clause C_i (s=+1 if v occurs negated, -1 if positive). Define the centered clause-falsification polynomial Q_F: {-1,+1}^n → R by Q_F(x) = Σ_{i=1}^m (∏_{v ∈ vars(C_i)} (1 + s_{i,v}x_v)/2 − 1/8), whose Fourier coefficient at T ⊆ [n] with |T| ∈ {1,2,3} is the closed-form sum Q̂_F(T) = (1/8) Σ_{i: T ⊆ vars(C_i)} ∏_{v ∈ T} s_{i,v} (computable directly from clause structure in O(m) time). Let r(F) := ||Q_F||_4 / ||Q_F||_2 ∈ [1, 3^{3/2}] be the Bonami-Beckner 4-2 ratio, and set G(F) := (3^{3/2} − r(F)) / 3^{3/2} ∈ [0, 1−3^{-3/2}]. Conjecture: there is an absolute constant c ≥ 0.05 such that for every unsatisfiable 3-CNF F, log₂(d*(F) + 1) ≥ c · n · G(F); a single F violating this inequality falsifies.

### Rationale

Hypercontractive saturation r(F) → 1 means Q_F has a heavy-tailed degree-3 spectrum: clause-violation behaves like a sharp witness function whose Fourier mass spreads thin, the regime characterizing expander-Tseitin and threshold-random 3-SAT where DPLL must explore exponentially many literal interactions. Conversely r(F) → 3^{3/2} (Khintchine-saturated) means Q_F is dominated by a few large coefficients, the regime of structurally simple unsat formulas that DPLL refutes shallowly. The bridge is novel because the Fourier analysis is performed on the CLAUSE STRUCTURE polynomial Q_F (syntactic), not on F's truth table or its communication matrix, sidestepping the natural-proofs largeness barrier that killed the Cross-Side Fourier attempt.
