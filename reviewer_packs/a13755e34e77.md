**Statement**:

> Fix m≥2 and let ζ_m=e^{2πi/m}. For f:{0,1}^n→{0,1} define α_f = (1/2^n)·Σ_{x∈{0,1}^n} f(x)·ζ_m^{int(x) mod m} ∈ Q(ζ_m). A single (f,s) pair with |N(α_f)| < s^{−100·φ(m)} refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> By Beigel–Tarui every ACC^0[m] function is a SYM of s ANDs, so α_f decomposes additively into s elementary character sums each with constant-norm contributions in O_K=Z[ζ_m]; sub-multiplicativity of the absolute norm under sums (via the Hadamard-style determinant bound on the regular representation) suggests the total norm cannot dip below s^{-O(φ(m))}. Random f have |α_f|≈2^{-n/2}, hence |N(α_f)|≈2^{-nφ(m)/2}, exponentially below the bound — exposing exactly the algebraic gap Williams' algorithmic method exploits between structured ACC computations and generic truth tables. The cyclotomic ring is the natural home for MOD_m structure, yet has not been used as a circuit-size obstruction.
