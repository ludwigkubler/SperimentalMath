**Field B** (complexity object): Padded permanent/determinant comparison on shared 0/1 support matrices (GCT det-vs-perm flavor)

**Statement**:

> For an n x n matrix M in {0,1}^{n x n}, let T_M = {sigma in S_n : M[i,sigma(i)] = 1 for all i} and define the q-major permanent and signed q-major determinant on the support T_M by perm_q(M) := sum_{sigma in T_M} q^{maj(sigma)} and det_q(M) := sum_{sigma in T_M} sign(sigma) * q^{maj(sigma)}, where maj(sigma) is the major index (sum of descent positions). Conjecture: for every n >= 3 and every 0/1 matrix M with |T_M| >= 2, the q=2 cancellation ratio R_2(M) := perm_2(M) / max(1, |det_2(M)|) satisfies R_2(M) >= sqrt(|T_M|) / (2*n). Equivalently, the sign(sigma)-weighted Mahonian sum on any 0/1-support exhibits at least square-root-of-support cancellation against the unsigned version, up to a linear factor in n.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Permanent vs determinant in GCT differs precisely by replacing sign(sigma) with 1 inside the S_n-sum; weighting both sums by q^{maj(sigma)} (a Schur-positive Hecke-algebra principal-specialization) refracts that difference through irreducible S_n-character spectra, and at q=2 one already sees [n]_2! = prod(2^k - 1) for perm_n but a much smaller signed sum for det_n. A square-root cancellation lower bound on every common support T_M would certify that determinantal coefficient sequences on permanental supports always look 'random-like' relative to their unsigned partners, giving a Mahonian witness for the per-vs-det gap that is preserved under support-preserving substitutions.
