**Field B** (complexity object): Algebraic Branching Program (ABP) width for homogeneous multilinear polynomials

**Statement**:

> For a homogeneous polynomial f of degree d in N variables, define σ(f) = dim{A ∈ gl_N : ∑_{i,j} A_{ij} x_j ∂f/∂x_i ≡ 0 as a polynomial}, the dimension of the Lie-algebra stabilizer of f under the standard GL_N derivation action. Conjecture: if f is computed by an algebraic branching program of width w (with affine-in-variables edge labels), then σ(f) ≥ N − 2w². Equivalently, the GL_N-orbit codimension obeys codim(GL_N · f) ≥ N − 2w², so any polynomial whose Lie-stabilizer codimension exceeds N − 2w² requires ABP width strictly greater than w.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> GCT's central thesis is that the symmetry (Lie stabilizer) of an explicit polynomial is the obstruction that separates orbit closures; det has a huge Lie stabilizer while permanent's is tiny, mirroring their conjectured complexity gap. Bounded-width ABPs build polynomials by 2×2-type recursive products and partial sums, which we conjecture force a large 'inherited' continuous symmetry algebra at least linearly in N once w is bounded, whereas random polynomials of degree ≥3 generically have σ=0. The mapping is purely differential-linear-algebraic (kernel of the polynomial-coefficient map on gl_N), so it avoids ring-only constructions that algebrize and is not a truth-table predicate, sidestepping Razborov–Rudich.
