## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Karlin–Edrei–Schoenberg total positivity / Pólya frequency theory (TP_k matrices, sign-regularity of 2x2 minors)
**Field B** (complexity object): Degree-2 SOS pseudo-moment matrix for Max-CUT on graphs (Goemans–Williamson regime), with a lifting interpretation via gadget composition

**Statement**:

> For a connected graph G=(V,E) on n vertices with combinatorial Laplacian L=D-A, let v_2,v_3∈R^n be the second and third eigenvectors of L (unit-norm, breaking ties by lexicographic sign). Define the rank-2 spectral pseudo-moment matrix M_G[i,j]:=(v_2[i]v_2[j]+v_3[i]v_3[j])/Z, where Z:=max_{a,b}|v_2[a]v_2[b]+v_3[a]v_3[b]|, and the TP_2 defect δ(G):=|{(i<j,k<l):det M_G[{i,k},{j,l}]<-1/n^3}|/binom(n,2)^2. Conjecture: there exist absolute constants c_1=0.5 and c_2=0.10 such that (i) every graph with max-CUT(G)/|E|≥0.879 satisfies δ(G)≤c_1·n^{-1/2}, and (ii) every graph on n≥12 vertices with max-CUT(G)/|E|≤0.55 satisfies δ(G)≥c_2. A single graph violating either inequality refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Pólya frequency / TP_2 matrices are exactly the moment matrices of log-concave one-parameter families; SOS-tight max-CUT instances (where degree-2 SOS already certifies a near-optimal cut) should correspond to log-concave 1D rounding directions and thus preserve TP_2 in the leading spectral block, whereas integrality-gap instances spread mass in a way that flips 2x2 minor signs. Because TP_2 is closed under coordinatewise polynomial gadget tensoring (Karlin 1968, Ch. 3), δ behaves as a lifting-friendly invariant: gadget composition can only grow δ by a constant factor, matching the 'preserved under polynomial reductions' requirement on the target property P. The bridge between Pólya frequency theory and SOS Max-CUT lower bounds appears to have under five papers (TP_k has been used in stable-polynomial proofs of Kadison–Singer but not in SOS degree-lower-bound machinery).
