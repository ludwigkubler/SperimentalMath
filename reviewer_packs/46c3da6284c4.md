# Stable-Rank of Layer-Symbol Matrix Separates Read-Twice BPs for IP_2
**Entry ID**: `46c3da6284c4`  **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`  **Recorded**: 2026-05-17 09:02:44 UTC

## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Forster-style stable-rank / numerical-rank theory (Forster 2002; Linial–Shraibman 2009) — a corner of sign-rank / discrepancy-based lower-bound machinery transplanted from communication matrices to the operator-valued symbol bundle of a branching program; this bridge has essentially no presence in BP literature outside the unrelated Nechiporuk rank tradition.
**Field B** (complexity object): Read-twice oblivious branching program size for IP_2 on 2n adversarial-ordered variables (Borodin–Razborov–Smolensky 1993 open separation), in the meta-complexity sense of seeking a 'compressibility witness' ρ(P) with ρ(P)=O(log size(P)) — a poly-time-computable structural invariant of the BP, NOT of its truth-table.

**Statement**:

> For a read-twice oblivious BP P of size s on 2n variables, with layers t=1..4n and transition matrices M_t(b)∈{0,1}^{s×s} for b∈{0,1}, form the layer-symbol matrix S(P) := [Δ_1 | Δ_2 | … | Δ_{4n}] ∈ ℝ^{s × 4n·s} where Δ_t := M_t(1) − M_t(0). Define the stable-rank invariant ρ(P) := ‖S(P)‖_F² / ‖S(P)‖_op². Conjecture: (i) ρ(P) ≤ 2·s for every read-twice BP P (numerical-rank anchor: this follows from rank ≤ s but we conjecture the tighter constant 2 holds for all syntactic BPs); (ii) for every read-twice BP P with output correlation ⟨f_P, IP_2_n⟩ ≥ ε over uniform 2n-bit inputs, ρ(P) ≥ c · ε² · 2^{n/2} for an absolute constant c > 0. A single instance with ⟨f_P, IP_2⟩ ≥ ε and ρ(P) < c·ε²·2^{n/2}, or any read-twice BP with ρ(P) > 2s, refutes it.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Each Δ_t records how flipping one read at layer t shifts the BP's state operator; for a small BP these shifts share an s-dimensional column space, capping the stable rank at O(s). IP_2's adversarial pairing of x_i with y_i forces the symbol bundle to encode 2^{Θ(n)} mutually-orthogonal cross-variable shift directions (after the middle cut, each pair (x_i,y_i) contributes a Hadamard-flavoured outer product), so the Frobenius mass spreads across exponentially many comparable singular values, making ‖S‖_F²/‖S‖_op² grow geometrically with n. The setup avoids natural-proofs (ρ is a structural property of P, not its truth-table), algebrization (singular values are analytic, not ring-arithmetic), and relativization (no oracle).
