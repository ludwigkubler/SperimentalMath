\begin{abstract}
We propose a research programme — not a result — to attack
formula-depth lower bounds via the language of large-scale geometry and
the Roe-style coarse cohomology of the Karchmer-Wigderson pair space.
Concretely: for a boolean function $f \colon \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}$,
let $X_f = f^{-1}(0) \times f^{-1}(1)$ equipped with the disagreement-
Hamming pseudo-metric $d_f$. We define a coarse depth invariant
$\kappa(f)$ via the Roe-trace pairing of 1-cocycles against
controlled operators on $X_f$, and outline five tentative axioms whose
combination would give a $\kappa$-based formula depth lower bound that
is, by construction, immune to the natural-proofs, relativization, and
algebrization barriers. We make explicit why this is currently a
\emph{programme}: ten sub-conjectures generated under the framework
have all returned \texttt{INCONCLUSIVE} (test crashes during
verification) or \texttt{BARRIER\_HIT} verdicts, none have been
empirically supported, and no lean-verified theorem is yet attached. We
provide a Lean~4 stub that records the primitives and axioms in
machine-readable form and lay out the explicit open problems that turn
the programme into a discipline.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why a programme, not a paper of theorems}\label{sec:why}

Formula-depth lower bounds for explicit boolean functions are blocked by
three barriers: \emph{relativization}~\cite{BakerGillSolovay1975},
\emph{natural proofs}~\cite{RazborovRudich1997}, and
\emph{algebrization}~\cite{AaronsonWigderson2008}. Almost every existing
bound is sub-quadratic in formula size; the long-standing open problem is
whether explicit functions admit super-logarithmic formula depth, which
would separate $\mathbf{NC^1}$ from $\mathbf{P}$. The Karchmer-Wigderson
characterization~\cite{KarchmerWigderson1990} expresses formula depth as
the communication cost of a two-party game on the pair space
$X_f$. Coarse geometry, in the sense of
Roe~\cite{Roe2003} and Yu~\cite{Yu2000}, provides invariants of metric
spaces that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item are \emph{coarsely invariant} (so they cannot distinguish a
        random $f$ from a structured $f$ at the Razborov-Rudich
        density level);
  \item are \emph{destroyed by oracle access} (oracle gates collapse
        $d_f$);
  \item live in the K-theory of the Roe algebra $C^*_u[X_f]$, which is
        not closed under polynomial extensions of the underlying
        alphabet.
\end{enumerate}
The first three properties are precisely those that the three barriers
exclude. The CG-KW programme aims to construct a formula-depth lower
bound from a coarse invariant that satisfies all three.

\textbf{What we have:} a precise definition of $X_f$, $d_f$, the
controlled-cover and Roe-pairing primitives, a target invariant
$\kappa(f)$, and five tentative axioms (\S\ref{sec:axioms}).

\textbf{What we lack:} a proof of any of the five axioms. The ten
sub-conjectures generated under the framework are listed in
\S\ref{sec:status} together with their current pipeline verdicts. None
is currently supported.

\section{Primitives}\label{sec:primitives}

Throughout, $f \colon \{0,1\}^n \to \{0,1\}$ is a boolean function and
$X_f = f^{-1}(0) \times f^{-1}(1)$.

\begin{definition}[KW-metric space]
The \emph{KW-metric} on $X_f$ is
\[
d_f((x,y), (x',y'))
\;=\;
\log_2 \min\bigl\{|S| \;:\;
S \subseteq [n]\,,\, x|_S \neq y|_S \text{ certifies } f(x) \neq f(y)\text{ or }
x'|_S \neq y'|_S \text{ certifies } f(x') \neq f(y')\bigr\}.
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Controlled cover]\label{def:cover}
A finite family $\mathcal{U} = \{U_i\}_{i \in I}$ of subsets of $X_f$
with diameter bound $R$ and multiplicity bound $m$: every $U_i$ has
$d_f$-diameter $\leq R$ and every $p \in X_f$ belongs to at most $m$
of the $U_i$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Coarse 1-cocycle]
A function $c \colon X_f \times X_f \to \mathbb{Z}$ of finite support
modulo controlled equivalence, satisfying the cocycle identity
$c(p, q) + c(q, r) = c(p, r)$ on every triple. Class in $HX^1(X_f)$ in
the Roe-style coarse cohomology.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Roe-controlled operator]
A bounded operator $T \in C^*_u[X_f]$ with finite \emph{propagation}
$\mathrm{prop}(T) := \sup\{d_f(p,q) : T_{pq} \neq 0\}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Asymptotic-dimension witness]\label{def:asdim}
A vertex coloring $\chi \colon X_f \to [k]$ together with a diameter
bound $R$ such that each colour class decomposes into $d_f$-bounded
pieces of diameter $\leq R$ that are pairwise $R$-disjoint. Witnesses
$\mathrm{asdim}(X_f) \leq k - 1$ in the Gromov sense.
\end{definition}

\section{The target invariant $\kappa(f)$}\label{sec:kappa}

\begin{definition}[Coarse depth]
\[
\kappa(f)
\;:=\;
\sup_{\substack{c \in HX^1(X_f),\, T \in C^*_u[X_f] \\ \langle c, T\rangle \neq 0}}
\frac{\log\bigl|\langle c, T\rangle\bigr|}{\log\,\mathrm{prop}(T)},
\]
where $\langle c, T \rangle$ is the Roe-trace pairing
$\sum_{p, q} c(p,q) \cdot T_{pq}$ with appropriate normalization.
\end{definition}

The programmatic claim is that $\kappa$ tracks formula depth.

\section{The five tentative axioms}\label{sec:axioms}

\begin{conjecture}[A1, Coarse-KW link]\label{ax:A1}
There is a constant $c_0 > 0$ such that for every boolean $f$,
\[
d(f) \;\geq\; c_0 \cdot \kappa(f),
\]
where $d(f)$ is the De Morgan formula depth.
\end{conjecture}

A1 is the load-bearing axiom; everything else is a sufficient condition
for $\kappa$ to be a non-trivial invariant on explicit functions.

\begin{conjecture}[A2, Composition sub-additivity]\label{ax:A2}
For all $f, g$ and the block composition $f \circ g^n$,
\[
\mathrm{asdim}(X_{f \circ g^n})
\;\geq\;
\mathrm{asdim}(X_f) + \mathrm{asdim}(X_g) - O(1).
\]
\end{conjecture}

A2 is a coarse-geometric analogue of the
Karchmer-Raz-Wigderson direct sum theorem~\cite{KarchmerRazWigderson1995}.

\begin{conjecture}[A3, Anti-natural-proofs]\label{ax:A3}
For a uniformly random $f$, $HX^1(X_f) = 0$ generically. Consequently
the predicate ``$\kappa(f) \geq T$'' is not a largeness property in the
Razborov-Rudich sense.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[A4, Anti-relativization]\label{ax:A4}
$\kappa$ is a metric invariant of $d_f$. Oracle access collapses $d_f$
to $\leq 1$, hence $\kappa$ to $\leq 1$, hence cannot relativize.
\end{conjecture}

\begin{conjecture}[A5, Roe-index rigidity]\label{ax:A5}
Nontrivial classes in $HX^1(X_f)$ detected by the Roe-trace pairing are
stable under coarse equivalence (in the spirit of Yu's coarse Baum-Connes
result~\cite{Yu2000} and Nowak-Yu's compendium~\cite{NowakYu2012}).
Andreev's function~\cite{Andreev1987} and element-distinctness are
conjectured to realize $\kappa = \Omega(\log^2 n)$.
\end{conjecture}

\section{Programme-level explicit-function targets}\label{sec:targets}

\begin{problem}[Andreev's function]
Prove $\kappa(\mathrm{Andreev}_n) = \Omega(\log^2 n)$. Combined with A1,
this yields a super-logarithmic formula depth lower bound on
$\mathrm{Andreev}_n$, separating $\mathbf{NC^1}$ from
$\mathbf{P}$ on this family.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}[Indexing function]
Prove $\kappa(\mathrm{IND}_k) \geq k - O(1)$ where $N = k + 2^k$. The
formula depth of $\mathrm{IND}_k$ is $\Theta(k)$, so this would match
the true depth up to a constant via A1.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}[Element-distinctness]
Prove $\kappa(\mathrm{ED}_n) = \Omega(\log^2 n)$.
\end{problem}

\begin{remark*}[An internal tension to resolve]
Two sub-conjectures (\texttt{ed6023df7533}, \texttt{2ee2e2f64a25}) were
flagged \texttt{BARRIER\_HIT} on the natural-proofs barrier despite axiom
A3 of the framework being explicitly an \emph{anti}-natural-proofs claim.
This is currently a contradiction: either (a) the proposer's barrier
filter incorrectly flags A3-compatible statements; (b) those particular
sub-conjectures violate A3 in a way the framework's verbal description
did not capture; or (c) A3 is too strong as stated. Resolving this
tension is the first concrete task on the CG-KW programme's to-do
list.
\end{remark*}

\section{What turns this scaffold into a paper of theorems}

The CG-KW programme converts the long-standing problem of formula-depth
lower bounds into seven concrete tasks of independent interest:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Prove the Coarse-KW link (A1) for at least one nontrivial class
of boolean functions.
\item Prove the asdim sub-additivity (A2), even with a $\log n$ slack.
\item Prove the anti-natural-proofs property (A3) by establishing
$HX^1(X_f) = 0$ generically — a Roe-algebraic concentration of
measure result.
\item Prove the anti-relativization property (A4) — likely the
shortest of the five proofs.
\item Construct an explicit cocycle-operator pair $(c, T)$ on
$X_{\mathrm{Andreev}}$ realizing $\kappa = \Omega(\log^2 n)$.
\item Repair the ten generated sub-conjecture tests
(\S\ref{sec:status}) and re-run them through the
five-gate pipeline.
\item Resolve the A3 tension noted above.
\end{enumerate}

A successful resolution of any single one of (1)--(5) would constitute
a publishable paper in its own right. The scaffold itself is presented
here in the format of Mulmuley-Sohoni's GCT programme~\cite{MulmuleySohoni2001}: a
proposed direction with explicit, testable, sub-conjectures, not a claim
of a result.
