**Statement**:

> For every binary symmetric channel with entanglement entropy H_E ≤ Θ(log n), the deterministic communication complexity CC_D(M) for an N×N Boolean matrix M is bounded by O(H_E^2 log^2 n). Equivalently: CC_D(M) = Θ(H_E^2 log^2 n).

> Quantum entanglement entropy provides a measure of the non-classical correlations in a quantum system.
