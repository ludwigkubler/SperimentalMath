**Field B** (complexity object): Tree-like resolution refutation depth d_T(F⊢⊥) of small unsatisfiable 3-CNFs, equivalently the minimum height of any DPLL search-tree certifying UNSAT

**Statement**:

> Let F be an unsatisfiable 3-CNF on n variables with m clauses, and let k_F:{0,1}^n→Z_{≥0} be the violated-clauses counter k_F(x)=#{j: x⊭C_j}, expanded as k_F=Σ_S k̂_F(S)χ_S in the Walsh-Hadamard basis. Define the dimensionless Fourier variance ratio R(F)=Var(k_F)/E[k_F]^2 over uniform x∈{0,1}^n (equivalently Σ_{S≠∅}k̂_F(S)^2 divided by k̂_F(∅)^2). Conjecture: every UNSAT 3-CNF F with E[k_F]≥1 satisfies d_T(F⊢⊥) ≥ ⌈log_2(1/R(F))⌉ + 1; a single F with d_T(F) strictly below this Fourier-concentration floor falsifies the claim.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> A small variance ratio R(F) means k_F is sharply concentrated around its mean, so the Friedgut/Bourgain noise picture predicts that random restrictions of small radius almost surely preserve at least one violated clause, forcing every branch of any DPLL tree to descend at least Θ(log(1/R)) levels before some clause empties. The integer-counter Fourier spectrum captures this concentration directly through Plancherel on k_F^2, bridging KKL-style spectral concentration with the proof-complexity primitive of search-problem decision trees in a manner distinct from prior Boolean-indicator influence bounds.

def generate_unsat_cnf(n, m):
    literals = list(range(1, n + 1)) + [-l for l in range(1, n + 1)]

def walsh_hadamard_transform(k_F):
    n = len(k_F)
    k_F.extend([0] * (2**n - len(k_F)))
    for s in range(1, n + 1):
        mask = (1 << s) - 1
        for i in range(2**(n-s)):
            j = i ^ mask
            k_F[i] += k_F[j]
            k_F[j] -= k_F[i]
    return [x / 2**s for s in range(n)]
