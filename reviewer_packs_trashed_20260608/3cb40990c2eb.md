## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Shifted partial-derivatives method in geometric complexity theory (Kayal–Saha–Tavenas 2016; Gupta–Kamath–Kayal–Saptharishi 2014): the dimension SPD_{ℓ,k}(p)=dim_Q span{x^β·∂^α p : |β|=ℓ,|α|=k} of the multiplicative-shift of partial derivatives, a coordinate-free arithmetic-circuit lower-bound functional computable in O(n^6) by rank of an explicit Sylvester-like lift matrix. It strictly extends Mignon–Ressayre Hessian rank (= SPD_{0,1} up to constants) and is the dominant algebraic technique used to prove super-polynomial homogeneous depth-4 bounds in the Mulmuley–Sohoni GCT program; rarely deployed in proof complexity.
**Field B** (complexity object): Tree-like resolution refutation leaf count L_T(F⊢⊥) of unsatisfiable random 3-CNF F at clause density α∈[4.5,5.5], equivalently lex-DPLL search-tree leaf count under unit propagation — the canonical proof-complexity proxy for refutation hardness.

**Statement**:

> For an unsat 3-CNF F on n≤16 vars, form the clause cubic Q_F(x_0,…,x_n)=Σ_{C=(a,b,c)∈F} ℓ_a·ℓ_b·ℓ_c where ℓ_i^+=x_i, ℓ_i^-=x_0−x_i, viewed in the monomial basis of Q[x_0,…,x_n]_3. Define ψ(F):=rank M_{1,1}(Q_F) − rank Cat_1(Q_F), where M_{1,1} is the (n+1)^2×(n+3 choose 3) coefficient matrix whose rows are the cubic coefficients of x_i·∂_j Q_F, and Cat_1 is the (n+1)×(n+2 choose 2) first-catalecticant. Conjecture: ψ(F) ≥ ⌊log_2 L_T(F⊢⊥)⌋ for every unsat 3-CNF F at clause density α∈[4.5,5.5]; a single instance with ψ(F)<⌊log_2 L_T⌋ falsifies it.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Q_F algebraicizes the unsat witness as a degree-3 form; multiplying first partials by linear shifts probes the ‘depth-4 expansion’ that Gupta–Kamath–Kayal–Saptharishi exploited for the 2^√n IMM bound, exactly the GCT-aligned nonlinear information missed by the Hessian alone. Subtracting Cat_1 isolates the genuinely shifted contribution and steers clear of the prior Mignon–Ressayre attempt on the blacklist. Tree-resolution leaf count is a sharp proxy for DPLL hardness, so a quantitative ψ-vs-log L_T link would expose unsat-instance hardness as a Sylvester-rank obstruction.
