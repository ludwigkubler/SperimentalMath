> Let M ∈ {0,1}^{N×N} with N ≥ 4 and define the rescaled spectral moments s_k := tr((MM^T)^k)/N^{k+1} for k=1,2,3,4, the 2nd and 4th free cumulants κ_2(M) := s_2 − s_1^2 and κ_4(M) := s_4 − 4 s_1 s_3 − 2 s_2^2 + 10 s_1^2 s_2 − 5 s_1^4, and the stable rank sr(M) := tr(MM^T)/||M||_op^2.
