## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Forman discrete Morse theory / mod-2 simplicial homology of the minimal-witness complex of a Boolean function
**Field B** (complexity object): Karchmer-Wigderson De Morgan formula depth D(f) for f:{0,1}^n→{0,1}

**Statement**:

> For every non-constant Boolean function f:{0,1}^n→{0,1}, let Σ_f^min be the simplicial complex on vertex set [n] obtained as the down-closure of the inclusion-MINIMAL witness sets W(x,y)={i:x_i≠y_i} ranging over pairs (x,y)∈f^{-1}(1)×f^{-1}(0), and let β(f)=Σ_{k≥0} dim_{F_2} H_k(Σ_f^min;F_2) be its total mod-2 Betti number. Then the De Morgan (Boolean) formula depth satisfies D(f) ≥ ⌈log_2(1+β(f))⌉. A single instance with decision-tree depth DT(f) < ⌈log_2(1+β(f))⌉ (which would imply D(f)<⌈log_2(1+β(f))⌉) falsifies the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> A KW protocol of depth d partitions the relation R_f into ≤2^d monochromatic rectangles, each labeled by a single witness coordinate i; restricting Σ_f^min to the vertices used by these labels yields a cover by ≤2^d stars (cones over a single vertex), and a Mayer-Vietoris / nerve argument forces β(f)≤2^d-1 whenever such a cover exists. The minimal-witness complex thus encodes a 'shape obstruction' that is invisible to rank-, Fourier-, or sensitivity-based KW techniques. Discrete Morse / simplicial F_2 homology of witness complexes has appeared in <5 papers crossed with formula depth, giving a genuinely fresh combinatorial-topological angle on KW games.
