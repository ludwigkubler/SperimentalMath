# Følner-Defect Floor Forces Logarithmic Communication Overhead on Equality-Lifted Gadgets
**Entry ID**: `723804a64233`  **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`  **Recorded**: 2026-04-28 12:42:37 UTC

## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Coarse Geometric Lifting (CGL) — coarse geometry / geometric group theory applied to communication complexity
**Field B** (complexity object): Deterministic two-party communication complexity CC(EQ_k ∘ G^n) of the lifted Equality function on n copies of a fixed MetricGadget G

**Statement**:

> Let G = (X,Y,g,d) be a MetricGadget with |X|=|Y|=k and Hamming-graph metric d, and let EQ_k : {0,1}^n → {0,1} denote Equality on n bits with Q(EQ_k) = n. Define the Følner defect of the lifted space at scale R as δ_R(n) = min over finite F ⊂ (X×Y)^n of |∂_R F|/|F|, where ∂_R F is the d_⊕-R-boundary. Then for every gadget G satisfying axiom A4 (Property A) and every n, CC(EQ_k ∘ G^n) ≥ Q(EQ_k) · ⌈log_2(1/δ_R(n) + 1)⌉ for R = diam(G), and equivalently CLC(EQ_k, G) is bounded below by n · log_2(AsdimCertify(LiftedInputSpace_n, diam(G))+1). In particular, when AsdimCertify returns multiplicity m at scale R, every deterministic protocol must use at least n · log_2(m+1) bits.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> This sub-conjecture directly tests Axiom A2 (Protocol → Cover) combined with Axiom A4 (Property-A Transfer). A2 says any cost-c protocol yields a RoeCover of multiplicity ≤ 2^c, so log_2 of the minimum cover multiplicity lower-bounds CC. A4 promotes that bound by the query complexity factor Q(f). For Equality, Q(EQ_k) = n and any non-trivial RoeCover at scale diam(G) of the n-fold lift must have multiplicity at least k^Ω(1) per coordinate by amenability obstructions (Følner defect), giving a per-coordinate logarithmic multiplier. The conjecture should hold because the Følner-defect quantity δ_R(n) shrinks geometrically in n for product metrics, forcing the cover multiplicity — and hence the protocol cost — to scale linearly with n with a logarithmic-in-k coefficient.
