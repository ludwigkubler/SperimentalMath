Let G(F*_v) be the simple unweighted graph whose nodes are the minterms of F*_v and whose edges connect pairs of minterms that share exactly k−1 vertices. Conjecture: for every such v, (i) the minterm count satisfies M(F*_v) ≥ C(v,k); and (ii) the average unweighted edge Forman-Ricci curvature κ̄(G(F*_v)) := (1/|E(G)|)·Σ_{(u,w)∈E(G)} (4 − deg(u) − deg(w)) satisfies |κ̄(G(F*_v)) − (4 − 2k(v−k))| ≤ 1. A single v for which either (i) fails or (ii) is violated refutes the conjecture.
