**Field B** (complexity object): Deterministic decision-tree depth Q^dt(f) of small Boolean functions f:{0,1}^k→{0,1}, lifted to deterministic two-party communication complexity D^cc(f∘IND_b) via the Raz-McKenzie / Göös-Pitassi-Watson indexing-gadget lifting theorem

**Statement**:

> For f:{0,1}^k→{0,1}, let π_f be the permutation of [0..2^k−1] obtained by stable-sorting the inputs by the key (f(x), x), and let λ(f)=(λ_1,λ_2) be the shape of the RSK insertion tableau P(π_f). By GPW lifting with indexing gadget IND_b, this entails D^cc(f∘IND_b) ≥ ⌈log_2(λ_2(f)+1)⌉·log_2 b − O(log k).

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Schensted's theorem makes λ_2(π_f) the length of the longest weakly-decreasing subsequence of π_f, a global obstruction distinct from antichain width, matroid rank, or Möbius invariants — it captures how thoroughly the value-class boundary scrambles the lex order.
