**Field B** (complexity object): Randomized two-party communication complexity of XOR-functions f(x,y)=g(x⊕y) accessed through the discrepancy-method lower bound disc(M_f)=||ĝ||_∞

**Statement**:

> For any non-constant g:{0,1}^n→{−1,+1}, let P_g={z∈{0,1}^n : g(z)=−1} and define the lattice star discrepancy D*_lat(g) := max_{a∈{0,1}^n} | |P_g ∩ [0,a]_⊓|/2^n − (|P_g|/2^n)·(2^{popcount(a)}/2^n) |, where [0,a]_⊓={z∈{0,1}^n : z≤a coordinatewise}; let ||ĝ||_∞ := max_{S⊆[n]} |ĝ(S)| be the spectral norm. Conjecture: there exist absolute constants c_1=1/8 and c_2=8 such that for every n≥4 and every non-constant g, c_1·||ĝ||_∞ ≤ D*_lat(g) ≤ c_2·||ĝ||_∞·log_2(n+1). A single (n,g) pair violating either inequality refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> The discrepancy lower bound on randomized XOR-communication is exactly log(1/||ĝ||_∞), yet ||ĝ||_∞ is a global Fourier quantity with no geometric face. Vertex star discrepancy is the canonical Chazelle–Matousek irregularity measure of a point set inside [0,1]^n; restricting test boxes to lattice corners makes it computable in O(n·2^n) via the zeta transform. If D*_lat ≈ ||ĝ||_∞, this gives the first concrete geometric (axis-parallel box) certificate of XOR-communication hardness, opening a Banaszczyk/Kashin-style geometric attack on unsaturated R(DISJ)-like bounds.

def spectral_norm(gh, n):
