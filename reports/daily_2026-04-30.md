## Kolmogorov Complexity of Shortest Resolution Refutations Bounded by Circuit Complexity

- **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`
- **Bridge**: Algorithmic information theory × Resolution proof length
- **Recorded**: 2026-04-30 02:07 UTC
- **Entry ID**: `4e25af61333f`

### Statement

For every unsatisfiable 3-CNF formula F on n variables with m = O(n) clauses, the plain Kolmogorov complexity of the lexicographically shortest resolution refutation π_min(F) satisfies K(π_min(F)) ≤ C ⋅ C_{	ext{det}}(f_F) + O(\log n), where C_{	ext{det}}(f_F) is the deterministic circuit complexity of the search problem f_F: {0,1}^n → [m] mapping assignments to violated clauses, and C > 0 is an absolute constant.

### Rationale

This bridges meta-complexity (via Kolmogorov complexity of proofs) with proof complexity by suggesting that hard proof systems do not generate algorithmically random derivations. The deterministic circuit complexity of the violation search problem is a natural proxy for the 'hardness' of certifying unsatisfiability, and by Hirahara-Oliveira's framework, such connections can avoid natural proofs if they reason about functionals of computations rather than predicates on truth tables.

## EF Refutation Size vs. Clause-Indicator Complexity

- **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`
- **Bridge**: Extended Frege / Frege systems × Clause-indicator polynomials
- **Recorded**: 2026-04-30 04:40 UTC
- **Entry ID**: `ece004bc4f32`

### Statement

For any unsatisfiable CNF formula F, the size of the shortest Extended Frege refutation of F is polynomially related to the complexity of the clause-indicator polynomial of F, measured by the number of monomials and their degrees.

### Rationale

The complexity of the clause-indicator polynomial reflects the intricate structure of the CNF formula, and Extended Frege systems can efficiently encode and manipulate this structure. Thus, it is plausible that the size of the refutation is related to the complexity of the polynomial.
