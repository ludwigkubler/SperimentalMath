## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Krawtchouk-polynomial spectral analysis: the slope at k=n/2 of the weight-distribution polynomial W_f(z)=Σ_{w=0}^{n} A_w(f) z^w of a Boolean function f, where A_w(f)=#{x∈{0,1}^n : |x|=w, f(x)=1}; the slope σ(f):=W_f'(1)/W_f(1) − n/2 is a classical coding-theory invariant (Delsarte/MacWilliams duality) very rarely deployed against circuit lower bounds.
**Field B** (complexity object): ACC^0[2] circuit complexity for Boolean functions presented by truth-table; specifically the minimum number S_2(f) of {AND,OR,NOT,MOD2}-gates in a depth-3 ACC^0[2] circuit computing f, the Williams/Murray target class for NEXP⊄ACC^0 separations.

**Statement**:

> For every Boolean function f:{0,1}^n→{0,1} with n≤20, |σ(f)|·log2(1+S_2(f)) ≥ Var_{x∼U}[|x| · f(x)] / (n+1), where Var is taken over the uniform distribution on {0,1}^n. Equivalently: any ACC^0[2] circuit of size s computing f must satisfy s ≥ 2^{Var(|x|·f(x))/((n+1)|σ(f)|)} − 1. A single (f,s) pair with s small but Var/((n+1)|σ(f)|) large refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> MOD2 gates can erase any single Krawtchouk coefficient, but the slope σ(f) is a *signed average* over all weights and is preserved up to a multiplicative log-size factor by depth-3 ACC^0[2] under random restriction (Smolensky-style approximation by low-degree GF(2) polynomials commutes with weight enumeration). The variance of |x|·f captures the layer-wise informational asymmetry that AND/OR gates can collapse only by multiplying fan-in, giving a Krawtchouk-vs-size trade-off in the spirit of Williams' algorithmic-method size bounds.
