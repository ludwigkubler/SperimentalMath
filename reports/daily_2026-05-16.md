## Layer-Commutator Frobenius Discrepancy Separates Read-Twice BP for IP_2

- **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`
- **Bridge**: Non-commutative operator discrepancy (Frobenius norm of layer-transition commutators, viewed as a Σ^b_1-witnessing measure in bounded arithmetic à la Cook–Krajíček) × Read-twice branching programs computing the inner-product-mod-2 function IP_2
- **Recorded**: 2026-05-16 20:13 UTC
- **Entry ID**: `0e06b5f9fc52`

### Statement

Let P be a read-twice branching program with state set S (|S|=s) on n Boolean variables. For each of the 2n read-positions r ∈ {1,…,2n} let A_r ∈ R^{s×s} be the averaged transition matrix A_r := (T_r^0 + T_r^1)/2, where T_r^b is the 0/1 matrix sending state u to state v if reading bit b at position r drives u→v. Define the layer-commutator Frobenius discrepancy ρ(P) := log_2( 1 + Σ_{1≤r<q≤2n} ‖A_r A_q − A_q A_r‖_F^2 / s^2 ). Conjecture: (a) ρ(P) ≤ 6·log_2(s) + 4 for every read-twice BP P; (b) for every read-twice BP P that computes IP_2 on n ≥ 4 inputs, ρ(P) ≥ (1/8)·n. A single P violating either inequality refutes the conjecture; together they would force size(P) ≥ 2^{n/48} for read-twice BPs computing IP_2.

### Rationale

Bounded-arithmetic witnessing of BP membership relies on local invariants that are Σ^b_1-definable from the BP layer data; commutator energies of averaged layer matrices are exactly that kind of computable witness, while operator non-commutativity is the algebraic signature of the bilinear structure IP_2(x,y)=⟨x,y⟩_2. Read-once BPs decompose into block-triangular layers where averaged transitions essentially commute on each reachable mass, but IP_2 forces every read-twice realization to mix x-reads and y-reads non-trivially. Because ρ is a Frobenius (L^2 spectral) quantity rather than a polynomial in the entries, the construction does not algebrize and the invariant is computed on BP structure (not the truth table), so it dodges Natural Proofs.

## Matroid Matching of Term Co-Occurrence Graph Bounds k-CLIQUE Monotone DNF

- **Verdict**: `INCONCLUSIVE`
- **Bridge**: Matroid matching theory (Lovász 1980 polymatroid matching theorem, applied to term co-occurrence graphs of monotone DNFs) × Monotone DNF size and Karchmer–Wigderson protocols for the k-CLIQUE indicator
- **Recorded**: 2026-05-16 20:46 UTC
- **Entry ID**: `02c57a92c88c`

### Statement

For a monotone DNF F = T_1 ∨ ⋯ ∨ T_m on n variables, build the *term co-occurrence graph* H_F = ([n], E_F) with E_F = {{u,v} : ∃ i, {u,v} ⊆ T_i}, and define μ(F) = match(H_F) / ⌈log_2(1+m)⌉, where match(H_F) is the size of a maximum matching in H_F. We conjecture: (i) μ is subadditive under DNF conjunction-expansion, μ(F∧G) ≤ μ(F)+μ(G); (ii) μ(F) ≤ 4 for every monotone DNF F of 'log-width' type, namely m ≤ n^c and max_i |T_i| ≤ c·log_2 n for any fixed c ≥ 1; (iii) for the canonical k-CLIQUE DNF F_{v,k} on v vertices with k = ⌊log_2 v⌋ (variables = the C(v,2) edges), μ(F_{v,k}) ≥ v/(2·(log_2 v)^2), i.e. unbounded as v → ∞.

### Rationale

Karchmer–Wigderson translates monotone formula size into communication on terms; matroid matching of the co-occurrence graph H_F is a representation-level polymatroid invariant capturing how 'expansive' the term overlap pattern is. Log-width poly-DNFs use few variables per term, keeping matchings tame, while every pair of vertices lies in some k-clique-term for k-CLIQUE, forcing H_F to be the complete graph K_v and matchings to scale linearly. Because μ reads the *syntactic* term structure rather than the truth table, it sidesteps Razborov–Rudich naturalness while staying squarely inside the KW monotone-protocol techniques.
