# Treewidth of Prime-Implicant Compatibility Graph Tracks DNF-MCSP

## 1. Conjecture
**Field A** (mathematical branch): Robertson–Seymour graph minor theory — graph treewidth tw(G) defined via optimal tree decompositions of bag-width ≤k+1, accessed by the equivalent vertex-elimination characterization tw(G)=min over orderings π of max_v |N>(v)| in the fill-in graph (Bodlaender 1996; Robertson–Seymour Graph Minors V; Arnborg–Corneil–Proskurowski 1987). A non-ring, set-comparison/min-over-orderings functional — treewidth is preserved under graph minors, NOT under polynomial ring lifts (treewidth of the fill-in pattern has no F_q polynomial surrogate), so the bridge is Aaronson–Wigderson algebrization-safe. <5 papers linking treewidth of prime-implicant intersection graphs to MCSP / minimum DNF size (extant treewidth work is on the input CNF's primal/dual/incidence graphs for SAT parameterized complexity à la Samer–Szeider, never on the PI compatibility graph as a meta-complexity invariant). Distinct from blacklisted FCA antichain width (a Galois-lattice width on the implicant context, not a graph-minor invariant), Mostar / Forman–Ricci / fatgraph / dismantlability (none uses tree-decomposition / elimination-ordering width), and the Mahler-measure single-number truth-table trap.
**Field B** (complexity object): DNF-MCSP — the minimum-DNF leaf size σ_DNF(f) of f:{0,1}^n→{0,1}, in the Masek 1979 / Umans 1999 / Allender–Hellerstein–McCabe–Pitassi–Saks / Hirahara meta-complexity regime targeting MCSP-style NP-hardness and crypto reductions; the canonical Quine–McCluskey set-cover formulation where σ_DNF is the optimum of a prime-implicant cover of minterms. The invariant tw(G(f)) is RELATIONAL (a graph parameter of the PI compatibility graph), not a single truth-table number, sidestepping the trap (j) that killed Mahler-measure.

**Statement**:

> For every Boolean f:{0,1}^n→{0,1} with at least two prime implicants, let G(f) be the prime-implicant compatibility graph: vertices = PI(f) (computed by Quine–McCluskey), edges {p,q} iff the partial assignments p and q are mutually consistent (no variable assigned to opposing values). Let τ(f) := σ_DNF(f) measure the minimum number of TERMS in any DNF for f. Then τ(f) ≥ tw(G(f)) + 1, where tw is graph treewidth; equivalently, every DNF representation of f has at least ω*(G(f)) := tw(G(f))+1 terms. A single instance with τ(f) ≤ tw(G(f)) falsifies.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Two prime implicants that are mutually consistent share a non-empty extension subcube, hinting that any minimum cover must 'separate' them by at least one term — the bag-overlap of an optimal tree decomposition of G(f) is the natural combinatorial obstruction to this separation. The Robertson–Seymour width parameters have been wildly successful in parameterized complexity (Courcelle's theorem, MSO-definability) yet completely absent from MCSP / minimum-DNF lower bounds, even though the prime-implicant cover problem is exactly the kind of monotone clutter problem where bag-overlap should obstruct cover size. The invariant is structural on PI(f) rather than on the truth table directly, and treewidth is comparison/set-based, dodging both algebrization and the truth-table single-number natural-proofs trap.
