## 1. Conjecture

**Field B** (complexity object): DPLL refutation tree size for Tseitin formulas on random cubic graphs

**Statement**:

> For every connected 3-regular graph G on n vertices with odd-sum Z_2-charge c (so Tau(G,c) is unsatisfiable), let mu_G(x)=sum_k (-1)^k m_k(G) x^{n-2k} be the matching polynomial of G and let M(mu_G)=prod_{r: mu_G(r)=0, |r|>=1} |r| be its Mahler measure. We conjecture that for every such (G,c) the median DPLL refutation tree size T(Tau(G,c)) over 30 random VSIDS-free variable orderings satisfies log_2 T(Tau(G,c)) >= log_2 M(mu_G) - 1, equivalently T(Tau(G,c)) >= M(mu_G)/2 for every odd-charge cubic Tseitin instance. A single (G,c) with T < M(mu_G)/2 refutes the conjecture.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Heilmann–Lieb constrains roots of mu_G to [-2√(d-1), 2√(d-1)] with multiplicities at the spectral edge controlled precisely by how 'tree-like' G fails to be, and Godsil's covering-tree identity makes M(mu_G) a quantitative measure of how many cycles obstruct a tree embedding. Since the Ben-Sasson–Wigderson width bound on Tseitin scales with edge expansion — which is exactly what forces matching-polynomial root mass near the spectral edge — the analytic complexity captured by Mahler measure should track DPLL depth. The matching polynomial has been deployed in statistical mechanics and zero-free regions of partition functions, but to our knowledge never as a Tseitin lower bound, giving a genuinely new bridge.
