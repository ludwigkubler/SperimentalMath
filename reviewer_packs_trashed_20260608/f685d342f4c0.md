**Field B** (complexity object): Boolean formula-depth complexity; the Karchmer–Raz–Wigderson (KRW) direct-sum conjecture for NC^1 lower bounds

**Statement**:

> For all Boolean functions f:{0,1}^n -> {0,1} and g:{0,1}^m -> {0,1}, let (X_f, d_f) and (X_g, d_g) denote their KW-metric spaces and let (X_{f∘g}, d_{f∘g}) be the coarse_product encoding the block composition f ∘ g. Then there exists a universal constant C ≤ 2 such that asdim(X_{f∘g}) ≥ asdim(X_f) + asdim(X_g) − C. Equivalently, certified by the asymptotic-dimension-witness primitive: if χ_f, χ_g are optimal (k_f, R_f)- and (k_g, R_g)-witnesses for X_f, X_g respectively, then no (k_f + k_g − C − 1, R)-witness exists for X_{f∘g} for any finite R. Combined with axiom A1, this yields the KRW-style implication d(f ∘ g) ≥ Ω(d(f)) + Ω(d(g)) − O(1).

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> This sub-conjecture tests axiom A2 (Composition sub-additivity) directly using the framework's coarse_product primitive. It mirrors the classical Bell–Dranishnikov inequality asdim(X × Y) ≤ asdim(X) + asdim(Y) for metric products, asserting that the matching lower bound also holds for the depth-adjusted KW-product metric d_{f∘g}. The mechanism is structural: a controlled cover of X_{f∘g} of multiplicity m must, when restricted to fibers, refine into controlled covers of X_f and X_g, so the multiplicity exponents add. Verifying A2 on small instances closes the gap between κ-additivity and KRW, since Andreev-style amplification then bootstraps κ-bounds through composition (Axiom A5).
