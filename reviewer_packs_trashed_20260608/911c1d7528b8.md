**Field B** (complexity object): Depth-d ACC^0[m] circuit size for monotone Boolean functions

**Statement**:

> For a monotone Boolean function f: {0,1}^n → {0,1}, define the rejection complex K(f) := {S ⊆ [n] : f(1_S) = 0}, which is a downward-closed abstract simplicial complex on [n] by monotonicity. Let μ(f) be the number of critical cells output by Benedetti–Lutz's randomized greedy discrete Morse matching on the Hasse diagram of K(f), reported as the median over 30 random seeds. Conjecture: for every fixed constant depth d and prime power m≥2, every depth-d ACC^0[m] circuit (not required to be monotone) computing f has size at least 2^{Ω((log μ(f))^{1/d})}; equivalently, if such a circuit has size s, then μ(f) ≤ 2^{O((log s)^d)}.

**Rationale (proposer's reasoning)**:

> Discrete Morse theory supplies a relational, Hasse-diagram-level topological invariant — pairings of faces with cofaces — that to our knowledge has zero prior application to ACC^0 lower bounds, and is structurally different from the truth-table single-number invariants (Mahler measure, magnitude, doubling) that have repeatedly triggered NATURAL_PROOFS.
